Book downsale «Ut av utbrenthet»: paste into Kajabi

Three emails that offer Maren's book (399 kr, free workbook) to people who got the whole sales sequence and did not buy the course. Buttons go to /ut-av-utbrenthet.

Send rules
  • Who: everyone who registered for the weekly webinar (event 2148837561) and did not buy, after the 8 sales emails (the last one is Wednesday 20:00).
  • When (Oslo time), in the week after their webinar: email 1 Friday 08:00 · email 2 Saturday 09:00 · email 3 Sunday 08:00. Thursday is a rest day after the last sales email. From the webinar start (Sunday 10:00) that is 118, 143 and 166 hours.
  • Never send to (checked before every email, also for people who bought the book long before they registered):
    • tag Kjøpt Ut av utbrenthet (id 2149603107; every book offer and the Edpay purchase add it)
    • product Fra stress til ro - skreddersydd for deg (id 2149607383; covers all its offers: every price, card, Vipps, Klarna, instalments)
    • tag stopp-bok (new tag; added when someone clicks «Klikk her» at the bottom of a book email)
  • Where: one Kajabi automation, not the event emails. Event emails cannot skip people who already have a tag; the automation checks before each send and keeps working when the event repeats every week.
    1. Trigger: Event is registered → event 2148837561.
    2. Wait: day of week Sunday 10:00 (their webinar start; someone who registers after the start waits for next week).
    3. Wait: day of week Friday 08:00 → Send email 1.
    4. Wait: day of week Saturday 09:00 → Send email 2.
    5. Wait: day of week Sunday 08:00 → Send email 3.
    6. Exit conditions (any one stops the automation): has tag 2149603107 · has product 2149607383 · has tag stopp-bok.
    Waits use the account time zone: check that it is Oslo/Berlin.
  • If Kajabi does not offer exit conditions (they need the «workflow sending controls» feature): put a branch in front of each email instead. Paths «has tag Kjøpt Ut av utbrenthet», «has product Fra stress til ro - skreddersydd» and «has tag stopp-bok» end the automation; the default path sends the email and continues to the next wait and branch.
  • Before the first send: build the page /stopp-bok (the «Klikk her» link goes there): a copy of /stopp-salg whose form adds the tag stopp-bok and does not remove anyone from the event.
  • First name: the HTML uses {{ first_name }}. Send yourself a test: if the name comes out empty, use {{ contact.first_name }}.
  • Free shipping: left out of email 3 until Maren confirms it.
